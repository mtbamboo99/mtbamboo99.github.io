🌕 2026 Mid-Autumn Festival / 中秋节 🌕

EVENT PERIOD / 活动时间 September 18 – October 2, 2026 Pacific Time (PT)
2026年9月18日 – 10月2日 以美国太平洋时间为准

Celebrate Mid-Autumn Festival with a special limited-time mascot! During the event, followers and subscribers can both use the 2026 Mid-Autumn mascot .

一起来过中秋节吧！ 活动期间会有限时特殊吉祥物登场， 关注者和订阅者都可以使用 2026 中秋限定吉祥物 。

🌕 View the mascot collection / 查看吉祥物收藏

🌕 Permanent Festival Collection / 永久节日收藏

Subscribers can also add the Mid-Autumn mascot to their permanent Festival Collection!

Simply use the Mid-Autumn mascot with Mascot Summon or Mascot Messenger at least once while subscribed during the event.

订阅者还可以将中秋吉祥物永久加入自己的节日收藏！ 只需要在活动期间保持订阅状态，并使用中秋吉祥物完成至少一次 Mascot Summon 或 Mascot Messenger 即可。

For example, select the Mid-Autumn mascot in chat with: 

!setmascot midautumn_2026

Then redeem Mascot Summon or Mascot Messenger while the Mid-Autumn mascot is selected. A confirmation message will be sent in chat when you've successfully unlocked it.

比如，可以先在聊天中输入上面的指令选择中秋吉祥物， 然后兑换 Mascot Summon 或 Mascot Messenger。 成功永久解锁后，聊天室中会发送确认消息。

You can also check your updated mascot collection with: 

!setmascot

也可以用这个指令查看自己目前可用的吉祥物和已解锁的节日收藏。

⚠️ Don't Miss the Event Window! / 请注意活动时间！

After the event ends, temporary access to the Mid-Autumn mascot will be disabled. Only subscribers who unlocked the mascot for their permanent Festival Collection during the event will retain it in their collection.

Festival mascots are not guaranteed to return in future events, so make sure to unlock it before October 2 if you want to keep it!

活动结束后，中秋吉祥物的限时使用权限会关闭。 只有在活动期间成功将它加入永久节日收藏的订阅者， 才会继续把它保留在自己的收藏中。

节日吉祥物 不保证未来会再次开放获取， 所以如果想收藏的话，记得在 10 月 2 日之前完成解锁！

Note: Festival Collection mascots are permanent unlocks, but can only be used while you have an active subscription .

注意： 节日收藏中的吉祥物为永久解锁， 但 只有在当前处于订阅状态时才可以使用 。
