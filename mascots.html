Standard Mascots / 常规Q版山竹

Standard mascots are the regular mascot choices available through the channel. Subscribers can choose their preferred mascot from the standard collection.

常规Q版山竹是频道中长期提供的基础款式。 订阅者可以从常规收藏中选择自己喜欢的Q版山竹。

The Regular mascot is also available to non-subscribers.
非订阅用户也可以使用 Regular Q版山竹。

Festival Collection / 节日收藏

Special festival mascots may appear during limited-time channel events. Each event may have its own unlock conditions.

节日期间可能会出现特别的限定Q版山竹。 不同活动的获得方式可能会有所不同。

Once a festival mascot is permanently unlocked, it stays in your Festival Collection. Festival Collection mascots can be used whenever you currently have an active subscription.

成功永久解锁节日Q版山竹后， 它会一直保留在你的节日收藏中。 当前处于订阅状态时，即可使用自己已经收藏的节日Q版山竹。

Permanent collection ownership and current mascot access are separate: your unlocked festival mascots remain in your collection even if your subscription ends, but they become usable again only while you have an active subscription.

永久收藏与当前使用权限是分开的： 即使订阅结束，已经解锁的节日Q版山竹也不会从收藏中消失； 重新处于订阅状态后即可再次使用。

Choose Your Mascot / 选择Q版山竹

First, check which mascots are currently available to you:
首先查看自己目前可以使用哪些Q版山竹：

!setmascot

Choose a specific mascot using its mascot ID:
使用Q版山竹的ID来选择指定款式：

!setmascot <name>

Or keep mascot selection random:
也可以让Q版山竹保持随机选择：

!setmascot random

Your available mascot list may change during special events. Use !setmascot anytime to check your current options.
特殊活动期间，你可以使用的Q版山竹列表可能会发生变化。 随时输入 !setmascot 即可查看当前可用的款式。
