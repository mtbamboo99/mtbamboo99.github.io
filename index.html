About / 关于我

🌱 Hi! I'm Bamboo!

Mandarin is my first language, and I'm also fluent in English. I currently live in the U.S.

This community is built for people who have lived multilingual and multicultural lives, and for those who genuinely appreciate and want to learn from those experiences.

Across Twitch and YouTube, you'll find all kinds of content. I play video games and make things by hand because those are the hobbies I enjoy. I also share my own experiences.

My interests tend to come in seasons 🎲. Rather than forcing myself to stick with one game or one kind of content, I built this channel to follow what I'm genuinely excited about. Some things become recurring favorites, some disappear for a while, and there's always something new to explore.

No matter the topic, what always comes first is bringing together people who value multilingual and multicultural experiences, and fostering a community built on curiosity and respect.
