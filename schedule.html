🌿 Chill streams are flexible! / 轻松类游戏直播视情况掉落！

Any scheduled slot on any day may occasionally be replaced by a Chill Game when I want something more relaxed and conversational.
如果山竹想要放松的话（包括但不限于被工作毒打、被生活毒打、生病等），任何一天的原定直播都有可能临时换成轻松类游戏。

How the Rotation Works / 轮换方式

🌲 Evergreen / 常驻

Games and activities I regularly return to between major releases and longer playthroughs. Evergreem != I play every week. I'll take a break every now and then.

会反复回归的常驻内容。常驻 != 每周都玩， 山竹经常会玩腻了就休息一段时间。

✨ Takeover / 新游

Major new releases or longer playthroughs can temporarily take over the corresponding CN or EN game slot. Once the playthrough is finished, that slot returns to its evergreen rotation.

新游发售或一些故事类游戏可以暂时接管对应的中文或英文游戏时段。 通关后，该时段会返回常驻。

🌿 Chill / 轻松

Relaxed, conversational, lower-energy games that can replace any scheduled stream slot on any day.

适合放松聊天、比较不费精力的游戏。因为山竹工作很累身体很虚，任何一天的直播时段都可能临时换成 Chill Game。

🎨 Crafts / 手作

Tuesday is usually my craft night. Projects rotate between Gunpla, miniature painting, and whatever I decide to make next.
周二通常是手作直播，会在高达模型、微缩模型涂装和其他手工项目之间轮换。
