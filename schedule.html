How the Rotation Works / 轮换方式

🌲 Evergreen / 常驻

Games and activities I regularly return to between major releases and story-driven playthroughs. “Evergreen” doesn’t mean I play them every week — I’ll take breaks and come back to them whenever I feel like it.

在大型新作和剧情向游戏之间会反复回归的常驻内容。“常驻”不代表每周都玩。山竹玩腻了就会休息一段时间。

✨ Takeover / 新游

Major new releases or story-driven games can temporarily take over the corresponding CN or EN game slot. Once the playthrough is finished, that slot usually returns to its evergreen rotation.

新游发售或一些剧情向游戏会暂时替换掉对应的中文或英文常驻游戏。通关后一般来说会回归常驻。

🌿 Chill / 轻松

Relaxed, conversational, lower-energy games for days when I need to take it easy. Any scheduled stream slot on any day can be replaced by a Chill Game.

适合放松聊天、比较不费精力的游戏。因为山竹工作很累身体很虚，任何一天的直播时段都可能临时换成 轻松类游戏。

🎨 Crafts / 手作

Usually rotate between Gunpla, miniature painting, and whatever I feel like making.

一般来说是拼高达、或者微缩模型涂装、或者其他心血来潮的手作项目。
